# Quick Design Spec: Draft Split 50/50

**Type**: Tuning
**System**: XP, Leveling, and Upgrade Drafts
**GDD Reference**: `design/gdd/xp-leveling-and-upgrade-drafts.md` — Draft Composition Rules
**Date**: 2026-05-16

## Change

| Parameter | Old Value | New Value | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| `normal_upgrade_weight` | `0.90` | `0.50` | More normal drafts should surface new weapons and items while weapon upgrade cards now roll from richer weapon pools. |
| `normal_new_gear_weight` | `0.10` | `0.50` | Prevent early drafts from feeling weapon-upgrade-only. |

## Tuning Knob Mapping

Maps to GDD tuning knobs `normal_upgrade_weight` and `normal_new_gear_weight`.
New values replace the prior approved range and keep the normal card family split symmetrical.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Normal draft cards roll `50%` upgrade and `50%` new gear before legality redirects.
- [ ] New gear still splits `50%` new weapon and `50%` new passive item.
- [ ] Upgrade rolls still split `50%` owned weapon upgrade and `50%` owned item upgrade.
- [ ] Page Event reward drafts still guarantee at least one legal new gear card.
